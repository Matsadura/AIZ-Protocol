"This is for eveyone"

Published on July 27, 2012

This is a clip from the 2012 London Olympics Opening Ceremony. It features Sir Tim Berners-Lee, the inventor of the World Wide Web, typing at a computer while sitting at a desk in the center of the stadium. As he types, the message "THIS IS FOR EVERYONE" appears in large, glowing letters across the stands, created by thousands of spectators holding up lights. It's a tribute to his decision to make the Web a free and open resource for the world.
